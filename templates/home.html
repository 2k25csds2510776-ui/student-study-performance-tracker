Welcome to Student Study and Performance Tracker

It is a platform where you can access the records of students.

It is created to help students see their performance based on subjects.


What You Can Track

  • 📊 Marks - You can track the academic performance of students across different subjects.
  • 📅 Attendance - You can monitor the attendance records of students over time.
  • 📚 Assignments - You can manage and track assignments for students across different subjects.
  • 📝 Exams - You can keep track of exam schedules and results for students.
